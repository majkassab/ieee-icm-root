Welcome & Event Overview

The 3rd Sino-Malaysia IEEE Circuits and Systems Society (CASS) Outreach Workshop is a key initiative designed to strengthen collaboration between academia, industry, and CASS members across China and Malaysia.


This one-and-a-half-day event brings together leading experts, researchers, and industry professionals to facilitate technical innovation and knowledge exchange in the field of circuits and systems. The program includes keynote technical talks, an industry visit, and a postgraduate mentoring session to deliver a comprehensive and engaging experience for all participants.
