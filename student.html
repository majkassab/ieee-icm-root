IEEE ICM 2026 Student Travel Grant Announcement

The ICM 2026 committee is offering Student Travel Grants ($200 – $700 USD) to assist student authors presenting their research.

📅 Important Dates

  • Application Deadline: October 30, 2026
  • Winners Notification: November 7, 2026

📋 Selection Criteria

  • IEEE Member Must be an active member.
  • Accepted Paper Must have an accepted paper at ICM 2026.
  • Student Status Undergraduate or graduate student.
  • Presentation Must present the paper at the conference.
  • Recommendation Must provide a recommendation letter from an advisor, chair, or senior colleague.

ℹ️ Key Details

Grant Value: $200 – $700 USD per grant (up to 10 awards anticipated; final amount depends on total eligible applicants).

Coverage

Travel-related expenses only (registration fees are excluded).

Policy & Review

Self-nominations allowed. Late applications will not be accepted. Applications will be evaluated by the ICM Conference Committee.
