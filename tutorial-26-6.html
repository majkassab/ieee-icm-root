Next-Generation Frequency-Reconfigurable RF Receivers Using Staggered N-Path Filters

Dr. Soumya Gupta

Soumya Gupta (Member, IEEE) received the M.S. and Ph.D. degrees in electrical engineering from Oregon State University, Corvallis, OR, USA, in 2021 and 2026, respectively. Since May 2026, she has been with Texas Instruments, Tucson, AZ, USA, where she is an Analog/RF Design Engineer working on high-speed amplifiers. She was a recipient of the Outstanding Scholar Program at Oregon State University from 2021 to 2023 and the Best Student Paper Award at the IEEE Radio and Wireless Week (RWW) in 2026. Her research interests include analog and RF integrated circuit design for wideband and interferer- tolerant wireless receiver architectures.

Dr. Gupta serves as a reviewer for the IEEE Transactions on Circuits and Systems I: Regular Papers (TCAS- I), the IEEE Transactions on Microwave Theory and Techniques (TMTT), the IEEE International Symposium on Circuits and Systems (ISCAS), and the IEEE Midwest Symposium on Circuits and Systems (MWSCAS).
