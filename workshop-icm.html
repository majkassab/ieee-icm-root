Hands-on Workshop on Chip Design and Fabrication

From Idea to Manufactured Silicon: A 2-Day Intensive Open-Source ASIC Design Flow

Date

December 9-10, 2026

Location

Borneo Cultures Museum, Kuching, Sarawak, Malaysia

Co-located with

38th IEEE International Conference on Microelectronics (ICM 2026)

Format

2-Day, Hands-on, Beginner-friendly

Capacity

Limited to 60 Selected Participants

Key Benefit

Qualifying designs will be fabricated into a physical chip via Tiny Tapeout & Open Silicon Initiative!

Apply for Workshop (Soon)

Workshop Overview & Highlights

This intensive two-day workshop introduces participants to the complete open-source digital integrated circuit (IC) design flow, combining transistor-level implementation with an automated RTL-to-GDSII workflow


Starting with an overview of the global IC ecosystem and open-source Process Design Kits (PDKs), participants will learn about community-driven ASIC fabrication initiatives like Tiny Tapeout, UNIC-CASS, Chipathon, and Code-A-Chip

Hands-On Experience

Transistor-Level Design

Implement a Boolean function from schematic simulation through layout, LVS, parasitic extraction (PEX), and post-layout verification.

Verilog & Automated RTL-to-GDSII

Develop and simulate a 4-bit counter in Verilog, pushing it through a full automated digital ASIC flow using open-source EDA tools.

Tapeout Integration

Integrate your design into the Tiny Tapeout platform for real-world chip manufacturing.

Key Learning Outcomes

  • Understand the Ecosystem Navigate the global integrated circuit landscape, open-source PDKs, and community tapeout programs (Tiny Tapeout, Chipathon, Code-A-Chip, UNIC-CASS).
  • Transistor-Level Implementation Build and verify a Boolean function from schematic through layout design.
  • Physical Verification Execute Layout Versus Schematic (LVS) checks, Parasitic Extraction (PEX), and post-layout simulations.
  • Digital Design & Simulation Write, test, and simulate digital logic (4-bit counter) in Verilog.
  • RTL-to-GDSII Automation Execute an automated open-source EDA digital flow.
  • Silicon Fabrication Readiness Package and submit finished designs to the Tiny Tapeout platform ready for manufacturing.

Participant Prerequisites & Eligibility

To ensure an optimal hands-on learning experience, applicants must meet the following prerequisites:

  • Language Proficiency: Advanced English (the course is fully delivered in English).
  • Technical Knowledge: Basic understanding of microelectronics and semiconductor principles, including transistors (BJT/FET) and CMOS technology.
  • Software Skills: Working knowledge of Linux operating systems, a scripting language, and Hardware Description Languages (HDL / Verilog).

What Selected Participants Receive

Supported participants selected for the workshop will receive:

  • Full Course & Certificate: Access to the complete 2-day IC design workshop and an official Certificate of Completion.
  • Fabricated Microchip: One physical manufactured chip per qualifying design, sponsored by the Open Silicon Initiative and produced via Tiny Tapeout.
  • Accommodation: 3 nights of shared hotel accommodation in Kuching.
  • Meals: Provided lunch and coffee breaks during the workshop days.
  • Conference Networking: Opportunity to attend IEEE ICM 2026 and engage with global semiconductor experts.

Note: Participant travel, visa fees, airport transfers, dinners, incidentals, and medical expenses are not included
