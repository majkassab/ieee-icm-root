Author Registration and Publication Policy

At least one author of each accepted paper must complete a Full Non-Student Author Registration by the author registration deadline.

Paper Coverage

  • Each accepted paper must be covered by a qualifying Full Non-Student Author Registration by November 7, 2026, either in person or through an approved online registration.
  • Student-rate registrations cannot satisfy the paper coverage requirement.

Multiple-Paper Registration

  • Each qualifying Full Non-Student Author Registration covers one accepted paper.
  • For multiple papers presented in person by the same author, full fees apply to the first and subsequent odd-numbered papers. A 50% discount applies to the second and subsequent even-numbered papers.
  • Additional-paper fees cover paper publication requirements only and do not provide additional conference registrations.

Publication Policy

  • At least one author must present each paper in person or through an approved online session.
  • Papers without valid registration or presentation will not be submitted to IEEE Xplore and may be excluded from the conference proceedings, subject to applicable IEEE publication policies.

Online Presentation Policy

Online presentation may be approved for authors unable to attend in person due to visa difficulties, health reasons, or insufficient travel funding.

  1. Approval: Submit a brief justification to the ICM 2026 Secretariat, with institutional or supervisor endorsement where applicable.
  2. Registration: Complete the applicable full online author registration by November 7, 2026.
  3. Presentation: Upload a 10-minute prerecorded presentation by the announced deadline.
  4. Attendance: Join the assigned online session and participate live in the Q&A and discussion. Approved online presentations will be scheduled in designated virtual sessions.
  5. No-show policy: Failure to attend the assigned session without prior approval may result in a no-show classification and may affect inclusion in the proceedings and submission to IEEE Xplore, subject to applicable IEEE policies.

Detailed presentation instructions will be provided through the official conference platform.

Registration Entitlements

Full in-person conference registration includes:

  • Access to technical sessions and tutorials
  • Conference proceedings
  • Conference lunches and coffee breaks
  • One welcome reception ticket and one gala dinner ticket
Workshop participation may require separate registration and additional fees. Details will be announced in the final conference program.

  • Conference proceedings or materials

Additional tickets are subject to availability.

Cancellation and Refund Policy

Cancellation requests must be submitted in writing to the ICM 2026 Secretariat.

  • On or before November 7, 2026: 50% refund of the registration fee.
  • From November 8, 2026: No refund.
  • Non-attendance does not qualify for a refund.
  • Applicable transaction charges may be deducted.
  • Approved refunds will be processed after the conference.

The same policy applies to additional social-event tickets.

Invitation Letter

  • Participants who require an invitation letter for visa or administrative purposes may request one after completing registration and payment.
  • Invitation letters do not guarantee visa approval or financial support.
  • Detailed instructions for requesting an invitation letter will be provided on the visa information page.
