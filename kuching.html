The Heart of Sarawak


Discover Sarawak & Kuching

Discover Sarawak, the Land of the Hornbills, where the heart of Borneo comes alive through rich cultures, ancient rainforests, warm hospitality and unforgettable flavours. At its centre is Kuching, a charming riverside city where history, culture and modern life meet.


Stroll along the Kuching Waterfront, Main Bazaar and Carpenter Street, and discover the Borneo Cultures Museum, Malaysia’s largest and Southeast Asia’s second-largest museum.


Sarawak Museum Kuching is also home to the world-renowned Rainforest World Music Festival, where music, culture and people from around the world come together against the spectacular backdrop of Mount Santubong and the rainforest.


Rainforest World Music Festival. As a UNESCO Creative City of Gastronomy, Kuching completes the experience with local favourites such as Sarawak Laksa, Kolo Mee, Midin and indigenous cuisine—making every visit a memorable journey through history, culture, music and food.

Kuching offers a unique combination of heritage, nature, music, culture and gastronomy, making it an ideal destination for discovering the diversity of Sarawak and Borneo.
